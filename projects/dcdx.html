Gen Z Research & Cultural Analysis

Tools: Google Slides, Google Sheets, Figma, Procreate


During my COVID-19 gap year, I served as Head of Research at dcdx, a Gen Z consulting startup. We provided research and marketing services to major clients including Snapchat, Chipotle, and Spotify. Beyond leading client-specific research projects, I contributed regularly to the company blog, offering insights on Gen Z trends and cultural patterns.

Click to download research deck

My research process, though still developing at the time (I've since developed a more rigorous approach), was largely self-directed. I would compile potential research topics and brief our founder, collaboratively deciding which ones to advance into full research projects.


The research phase began with web scraping social media discourse, reviewing academic literature, and analyzing broader cultural conversations. This initial exploration helped me develop targeted research questions for our growing network of Gen Z ambassadors (known as "the Loop").

1. Topic Development

Social media analysis + academic research

2. Data Collection

Surveys, interviews, focus groups

3. Analysis & Synthesis

Insights → compelling narratives

Data collection involved multiple formats: Google Forms questionnaires, group Zoom sessions, and one-on-one interviews. Using Google Sheets, I consolidated and analyzed both quantitative and qualitative data.


The final step was transforming these findings into compelling narratives for our clients. This involved synthesizing the research into various formats - decks, articles, emails - while incorporating graphic design, data visualization, and strategic copywriting.

Key Outcomes: Successfully delivered actionable insights to Fortune 500 clients, helping them understand and connect with Gen Z audiences. My research directly informed marketing strategies and product decisions for major brands.


What I learned: This experience taught me the importance of balancing cultural intuition with rigorous methodology. While my approach was still developing, the project highlighted my ability to identify emerging trends and translate complex cultural patterns into accessible business insights.
